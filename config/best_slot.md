# Publish time: how it was chosen

- **Long episodes** (TUE, FRI): 23:30 UTC (02:30 Istanbul), unchanged
- **Shorts** (MON, WED, THU, SAT, SUN): 23:30 UTC (02:30 Istanbul), unchanged
- **Best candidate times (UTC, score):** 23:30 0.642, 23:45 0.642, 23:15 0.640, 23:00 0.636, 22:45 0.631
- **Audience used:** typical English-language history channel (US 50 %, GB 13 %, IN 9 %, CA 7 %, AU 6 %); no channel analytics yet, so no learning from real views.

Weekdays are fixed in config/schedule.json. Videos already scheduled keep their time.
