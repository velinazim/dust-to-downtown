## Simplified traces (≈) — expect errors of 100–300 m

- **Themistoclean wall**: polygon through the seven TT gates, over the Hill of the Muses/Pnyx ridge on the west (WP:
  wall ran through the Kerameikos; Philopappos hill inside the circuit's SW).
- **Long Walls**: North wall and Middle (south) wall from the west side of the city to Piraeus, ~180 m apart, through
  the excavated Tavros point; Phaleric wall from the south of the city to the shore at Phaleron. Ends checked on the map.
- **Piraeus walls**: land-side loop around the peninsula (≈).
- **Post-Herulian wall** (EIE): Acropolis NW corner → north along the Panathenaic Way → Stoa of Attalos → south wall of
  Hadrian's Library → ~350 m east → south back to the Acropolis.
- **Haseki wall** (WP): Odeon of Herodes → Theatre of Dionysus → Arch of Hadrian → Amalias → Syntagma → Stadiou → old
  National Bank (Aiolou/Sophokleous) → Koumoundourou Sq. → Theseion → Areopagus → Odeon. My trace is shorter than the
  quoted ≈10 km, so no length on screen.
- **Districts**: Bronze Age/Archaic settlement round the Acropolis, the classical city filling the wall, Piraeus, Roman
  east quarter (Olympieion/Ilissos), the small late-Roman town, the Byzantine/Ottoman town inside the later Haseki line,
  the 1833 triangle (Omonia–Kerameikos–Syntagma, BRIT/searchculture), 19th-c. growth, refugee towns (EKT names;
  positions approximate), the post-war basin (bounded by Aigaleo, Parnitha foothills, Hymettus and the sea), Ellinikon
  redevelopment. All outlines are plausible simplifications.
- **Ancient Agora** kept open the whole film (it was built over in medieval/Ottoman times and excavated from 1931 —
  simplification).
- **Railway** Thiseio–Piraeus drawn as a straight-ish line.
- **Parthenon after 1687**: drawn as a lower, roofless ruin.
