# Berlin — research notes

Episode: ~15 min, 7th century → 2026. All pages below were opened during research on 8 Nov 2026 (full list in
`_sources.json` / city.json `sources`). Each URL was taken from a web-search result and read through the web-fetch agent.
Abbreviations: HB = History of Berlin (WP), TB = Timeline of Berlin (WP), CO = Cölln (WP), DEM = Demographics of Berlin
(WP), CAV = Berlin population statistics (Cavacopedia mirror), STAT = Amt für Statistik Berlin-Brandenburg press
release 73/2026, BR = Britannica "Berlin: History", BLN = berlin.de "The medieval trading center", GBB = berlin.de
"Greater Berlin Act", GBA = Greater Berlin Act (WP), SM = Berliner Stadtmauer (DeWiki) + Landesdenkmalamt page,
FST = Berlin Fortress (WP), CW = Berlin Customs Wall (WP), HOB = Hobrecht-Plan (WP), BOMB = Bombing of Berlin in WWII
(WP), BLK = Berlin Blockade (Wikiwand), BW = Berlin Wall (WP), MM = Mauermuseum facts & figures, BUREG = German
Federal Government "BER takes off". Landmark pages are named in the landmark table.

Note: for the long pages (HB after the 1920s, TB after 1880, Tempelhof, Hauptbahnhof, Potsdamer Platz) the fetch tool
returned the first ~40,000 characters verbatim and a summary of the rest. Facts taken only from such a summary are
marked (S); on-screen facts were preferred from verbatim parts.

Wall courses, district outlines, the Tiergarten / Tempelhof / forest areas and the 1961 Wall ring are **simplified
traces** (marked ≈) drawn by hand between known points and streets named in the sources (Littenstraße, Waisenstraße,
Spreekanal/Friedrichsgracht, Schleusenbrücke, Torstraße, Ebertstraße, Stresemannstraße, Marchlewskistraße,
Friedenstraße, the Ringbahn); expect errors of ~100–300 m in the centre and up to 1–2 km for the outer city limits and
the outer West Berlin border. OpenStreetMap is only used by the Preview action; traces are checked against
`preview/map_core.png` / `map_full.png` and corrected there.

## Traces (≈) and simplifications

- Cölln (island south of the palace) and Alt-Berlin (north-east bank): drawn from the medieval wall description (SM):
  Berlin side along Waisenstraße/Littenstraße to the Stralauer Tor, north to the Spandauer Tor at the end of Spandauer
  Straße; Cölln side along the Spreekanal (Friedrichsgracht) to the Schleusenbrücke and back east to the Spree.
- Fortress (1650/1658–1740s): 13 bastions (FST), drawn as a ring ~150–250 m outside the medieval wall including
  Friedrichswerder; individual bastions are not drawn.
- Customs wall (1737–1868): Torstraße, Hannoversche/Charitéstraße, Ebertstraße, Stresemannstraße, the gates Brandenburg,
  Potsdam, Anhalt, Halle, Kottbus, Silesian (Spree), Oberbaum, Stralau, Frankfurt, Landsberg, Prenzlau, Rosenthal,
  Oranienburg (CW); the 1737 line and the 1800s additions are drawn as one line (simplification).
- Friedrichstadt, Dorotheenstadt: rectangles between the Spree, Behrenstraße, Ebertstraße and the fortress/Lindenstraße.
- Wilhelmine ring 1862–1914: drawn inside the Ringbahn line (Hobrecht Plan area, HOB).
- Greater Berlin outline (1920): hand-traced city limits, ±1–2 km.
- WWII damage: buildings fade (≈ a third lost in the core and ring), then a postwar district re-grows on the same land
  1950–1975 (BOMB: "about a third of Berlin's area badly damaged").
- Berlin Wall: one line, inner-city section along Bernauer Straße – Reichstag – Brandenburg Gate – Potsdamer Platz –
  Checkpoint Charlie – the Spree – Treptow, then the outer ring around West Berlin (≈ ±1–2 km). The engine draws it as a
  wall; the double "death strip" is not drawn.
- Postwar housing estates are drawn as one generic district 1955–1990 without names on screen (their dates were not
  researched).
- Spandau and Köpenick: small Slavic-era clusters only (≈ positions of the old towns); no dates beyond "7th/8th c.".
- Rivers: all area-mapped rivers from OSM (in Berlin many Spree water areas carry no name, so a `rivers_keep` name filter dropped the central Spree); canals (Landwehrkanal, Spreekanal) are too thin and are left out;
  natural lakes (Müggelsee, Tegeler See, Wannsee) are part of the base map from the start.

## Left out / uncertain

- 1376 fire, the Mühlendamm, the date of the first Spree bridge: not found in the sources → not shown.
- Berlin's "founding" by Albert the Bear in 1163 (TB, "approximate date") conflicts with HB → not used.
- Death tolls at the Wall (136 to more than 200, BW) and of the 1953 uprising: not on screen.
- Holocaust: Britannica gives Berlin's Jewish population as 170,000 (1925) and says 5,000 survived in Berlin (undated) →
  not shown as a number; Kristallnacht is the only on-screen event (neutral wording).
