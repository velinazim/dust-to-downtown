# Vienna — research notes

Episode: ~15 min, AD 97 → 2026. Every on-screen date/number below has its source; all pages were opened during research
on 4 Nov 2026 (full list in `_sources.json` / city.json `sources`). Abbreviations: HV = History of Vienna (WP),
TV = Timeline of Vienna (WP), VWP = Vienna (WP, demographics table, via EncycloReader), VIN = Vindobona (WP),
BRIT = Britannica "Vienna: History" / "The people", AEIOU = Austria-Forum "Wien, Geschichte" / "Wien (English)",
STAT = Statistics Austria press release 9 Feb 2026, SVD = St Stephen's Cathedral (WP, IPFS mirror).

Landmark coordinates are the published coordinates of each page (Wikipedia infobox unless noted). Wall courses,
district outlines, camps and the Danube Island strip are **simplified traces** (marked ≈) drawn by hand between known
points (Graben, Tiefer Graben, Rotenturmstraße, the Ring, the Gürtel, the Danube Canal); expect errors of ~100–300 m.
OpenStreetMap is not reachable from the Claude session; traces are checked against the preview map
(`preview/map_core.png`, `map_full.png`) and corrected there.

## Geography and simplifications (≈)

- **Danube:** OSM area water named "Donau" (main Danube, Danube Canal, New Danube); Alte Donau is a lake and is shown
  throughout (it was the main stream before 1875). The main Danube is drawn in its modern straightened bed for the whole
  film — before 1870–1875 the river ran in many arms across the plain; this is a simplification and is not stated on screen
  except in the regulation segment. The New Danube channel is to be flooded only from 1988 (timed_water, traced from the
  real shoreline after the first preview); until then the 450 m floodplain/Danube Island strip is open land (open space).
- **Vindobona:** ≈20 ha rectangle between Tiefer Graben, Graben, Rotenturmstraße and the Salzgries terrace (VIN names
  these streets). Canabae south/west of the camp and the civil town in the 3rd district (VIN) are simplified areas.
- **Walls:** medieval wall ≈ just inside today's Ring, closed along the Danube Canal; the 1548 bastion fortress is the same
  ring drawn 6 % larger with a moat; both removed for the Ringstraße (1857–1860s). Linienwall ≈ just inside the Gürtel, from
  the canal at St Marx to Lichtental (Linienwall WP gives the ends and the 13.5 km length).
- **Districts:** early medieval settlement inside the old camp; Babenberg town; walled city c. 1200; thin early suburbs that
  are thinned in 1683 (defenders demolished houses around the walls — Battle of Vienna WP); Baroque suburbs inside the
  Linienwall (1690–1830); Leopoldstadt; Ringstraße zone (1860–1890); Gründerzeit suburbs; Vororte (joined 1890–1892);
  Floridsdorf (1904); postwar suburbs; Donau City, Seestadt and the Hauptbahnhof quarter as modern areas. Years of growth
  inside each area are visual (the engine spreads buildings across from→to).
- **Camps 1529 / 1683:** army tents placed west and south of the city; positions are illustrative only (no camp
  outline in the sources opened besides "main camp further south" and the Türkenschanze for 1683).
- **Landmarks without published coordinates:** Parliament (≈ between City Hall and Volksgarten), Neue Burg (≈ south-west
  wing of the Hofburg), Gloriette (≈ on the hill south of the palace), Northern Railway station (≈ Praterstern), Scots
  Abbey (≈ Freyung 6), Vienna International Centre second block (≈). Rise start years for DC Tower 1 (2011) and the Danube
  Tower (Oct 1962, sourced) are animation starts.
- Reichsbrücke I is removed in 1934 when the second bridge was begun (WP: second bridge built 1934–1937).
