## Reconstructions (≈)

- Penn's grid: Front St at -75.1425; Broad St through City Hall; 13 equal blocks between them (Independence Hall at
  5th–6th comes out right). Vine 39.9575, Market 39.9520, South 39.9415 (old city limits Vine–South per the Consolidation page).
- Districts: first houses Front–3rd St (1682–1700); colonial city east of 7th St (1700–1776, "clustered east of 6th Street");
  Northern Liberties and Southwark 1730–1800; west to Broad 1790–1830 and to the Schuylkill 1815–1855 (Workshop of the
  World: early 19th c.); rowhouse city 1854–1925; outer neighbourhoods 1900–1975; Penn Center 1953–1970; towers 1986–2020.
  Swedish farms at Wicaco 1660–1700 drawn as a sparse cluster around Gloria Dei. Camden (New Jersey) drawn as plain
  background town 1850–1930 (not dated on screen). County outline simplified.
- British camps 1777–1778 drawn north of Vine St (exact positions not in the opened sources).
- The five squares are placed from the grid (≈ size).
- Markets St El route west of the Schuylkill ≈ along Market St; PRR viaduct ("Chinese Wall") 1881–1953 ≈ along Market–Filbert.
