# Edinburgh — research notes

Episode: ~14 min, c. 1000 BC → 2026. Research 10 Nov 2026. Every on-screen date/number below has its source; all pages were
opened during research (36 pages, full list in `_sources.json` / city.json `sources`). Short names used below:
BrH = Britannica "Edinburgh: History"; BrC = Britannica "Edinburgh Castle"; CB = Edinburgh Castle blog (HES);
WP-x = Wikipedia article x. Street lines, district outlines, open spaces and camps are simplified traces (marked ≈).
Not opened: en.wikipedia "Timeline of Edinburgh history" (PROVENANCE_REQUIRED on the redirect) — nothing is taken from it.
The WP "Edinburgh Castle" article was read verbatim only for its first 40k characters; items only in the model summary of the
rest (1650 three-month siege, 1689, 1745 details, War Memorial 1927, Half Moon Battery 1573–88) are NOT used on screen,
except 1650/1745 where BrC states the same thing.

## Reconstructions (≈)

- Royal Mile drawn as a straight-ish line castle → St Giles' → Netherbow → Holyrood; early burgh = a strip along it; Canongate
  strip from Netherbow to Holyrood; Cowgate/Grassmarket filled from 1450.
- Flodden Wall traced through the sourced points above, via West Port, the Vennel, Greyfriars, Bristo/Potterrow, Drummond St,
  Pleasance, Cowgate Port, St Mary's/Leith Wynd and Netherbow to the New Port under North Bridge; removed by 1785 (ports
  removed 1764–1780s). King's Wall along the south side of the High Street above the Cowgate to St Mary's St (removed ≈1560,
  replaced by Flodden). Telfer Wall Vennel → Lauriston Place → Bristo St → Bristo Port, removed ≈1762 (bastions demolished 1762).
- Nor Loch: water from ≈1420 ("early 15th century") to 1765 in the valley of Princes Street Gardens (≈ outline).
- Leith: harbour town at the Water of Leith mouth; 1548–1560 bastioned ring ≈ (shape unknown, simplified), removed ≈1561
  (not dated on screen); besiegers' camp 1560 south of Leith ≈.
- New Town outlines from the streets (Princes St, Queen St, St Andrew Sq, Charlotte Sq; grid ~20° north of east); second New Town,
  Moray estate, west end and Calton terraces ≈. Victorian ring and 20th-century suburbs ≈ (council area outline simplified).
- Great Fire area 1824 ≈ (High Street south side, Old Assembly Close to Parliament Close).
- Railway lines through the valley ≈.
- Base map: the terrain tiles put gently sloping "land" 1–1.5 km out into the Forth (elevations 1–5 m north of Leith, where the
  shore is). `geo.sea_level_m` = 6 puts the coast at Leith/Newhaven/Seafield/Portobello where the city actually meets the sea
  (checked against the Leith and Duddingston/Lochend lakes positions in the map). The Water of Leith is NOT drawn (as a geo_edits river it rendered as a wide sand ribbon).
  Land north of the old shore at Leith/Newhaven (modern dock and harbour reclamation, ≈ outline, undated) is kept as sea; a strip
  well inside the shore is forced to land so the 6 m threshold leaves no ponds behind the beach (≈).
- Elevation tiles are smoothed (Castle Rock and Arthur's Seat lower than real); vertical exaggeration 3 as in other episodes.
