## Walls and districts (≈ reconstructions)

- Old Town wall + moat: along today's Národní – Na Příkopě – Revoluční (OT: the moat "is now covered up" by these
  streets), river to river. OT says the moat and wall were dismantled after the New Town was founded, without a year →
  drawn until 1784 (unification) as a simplification.
- New Town wall: ~3.5 km, 6–10 m high, four gates (NT), Vyšehrad → ridge → river at Těšnov (≈ course). Removed with the
  fortifications from 1874 (RAD74; demolition ran to the 1920s — drawn as one step in 1874).
- Lesser Town wall (≈ course), Hradčany wall (≈), Hunger Wall (≈ course over Petřín, still standing).
- Vyšehrad: settlement 10th c. (≈ oval), castle, Baroque ramparts from 1654.
- Suburb footprints and dates: Karlín 1817 (BRM); Smíchov, Vinohrady (town 1879), Žižkov (town 1881), Holešovice-Bubny
  (joined 1884), Libeň (1901), Greater Prague 1922 (PCA); shapes ≈. Panel estates dates from PCA/JM; shapes ≈.
- Fires: 1541 Lesser Town/castle (MS), 1689 Old Town + Jewish Town (OT), National Theatre 1881 (NTH), 1945 bomb site of
  the Dancing House (BOMB/DH). Smoke: Vítkov 1420, Lesser Town/bridge 1648, Vinohrady/Charles Square 1945.
- 1420 crusader camp: north of the river (≈ position; VIT gives no camp site) — shown only for a moment, not labelled.
