## Simplifications and uncertainties

- Aurelian Walls: traced through the 11 gate coordinates above; the stretches between gates (Castra Praetoria corner,
  Porta Metronia/Latina, the Testaccio end and the river wall along the Campus Martius) are ≈. The wall stays standing
  to today (large parts survive); the 1870 breach is shown with smoke only.
- Servian Wall: no gate coordinates exist on the source page; the line is ≈ round the Capitoline, Quirinal, Viminal,
  Esquiline, Caelian and Aventine from the gate order given, ending at the Tiber by the Forum Boarium. Shown until AD 300
  (superseded; fragments survive).
- Leonine Wall: ≈ around the Borgo and Vatican hill, from Castel Sant'Angelo; shown from 848 (four years of work under
  Leo IV, 847–855) — the on-screen text does not give exact years.
- Pons Sublicius: exact site unknown ("near the Forum Boarium, downstream of Tiber Island"); drawn ≈ just below the
  Pons Aemilius and removed by 800 (≈). Pons Cestius drawn ≈ west of the island (built between 62 and 27 BC, shown 46 BC).
  Modern bridges (1880s–1950s) are not drawn — no coordinates were opened for them.
- Bridge ends and wall ends are placed from the real river in geo.json (checked in preview); the Tiber's pre-1876 banks
  (before the embankments) are not modelled.
- Landmark sizes: from the sources where given (Colosseum, Circus, Trajan's Forum, Caracalla, Mausoleum of Augustus,
  St Peter's, Lateran, Vittoriano); others (Temple of Jupiter — disputed 60 × 60 m —, Theatre of Pompey, Hadrian's tomb,
  Old St Peter's, Santa Maria Maggiore) are modest ≈ sizes. Theatre of Pompey is a simple block.
- Removal dates that no source gives are ≈ visual choices: Temple of Jupiter ruin by 800; Circus Maximus stands gone by 1100;
  Old St Peter's shown removed in 1506 and the new basilica rising slowly from 1506 to the 1590 dome (in reality the old church was demolished in stages); Theatre of Pompey gone by 700 and Circus Maximus stands by 1100 (≈).
- Districts (Palatine huts, archaic city, Republican city, Imperial city, medieval abitato in the Tiber bend, Borgo,
  Renaissance/Baroque growth, post-1871 quarters, interwar and post-war suburbs, EUR) are ≈ areas for symbols, not surveyed
  boundaries. The medieval shift to the Tiber bend is from Carleton CGMR (Development of the Disabitato).
- Fires: 390 BC ≈ the archaic city except the Capitol; AD 64 ≈ the Circus valley, Palatine, Caelian and Esquiline
  (the source gives 10 of 14 districts, not boundaries).
- Smoke only (no burned area) for 410, 455, 846 (St Peter's), 1084, 1527 and the 1870 Porta Pia breach.
- GRA ring road drawn ≈ as a circle 11.4 km from the Capitol (its design rule), from 1970.
- Via Appia ≈ from the Circus Maximus past the Baths of Caracalla to Porta San Sebastiano and straight south-east;
  Via Flaminia ≈ along the Via Lata (Via del Corso) to Porta del Popolo and Ponte Milvio.
