# Amsterdam — research notes

Episode: ~14 min, c. 1000 → 2026. Every on-screen date/number below has its source (all pages opened during research,
2 Nov 2026; full list in `_sources.json` / city.json `sources`). Landmark coordinates are the published coordinates of
each page (Wikipedia infobox unless noted). Canal lines, the medieval shore of the IJ, district outlines, fill zones and
fire areas are simplified traces between known points (marked ≈). OpenStreetMap/Overpass and the elevation tiles cannot
be reached from the Claude session, so the traces were drawn by hand and checked against the preview map
(`preview/map_core.png`); expect errors of ~50–150 m.

## Population on screen
c. 1000–1299: no figure ("—"). Anchors: 1300 1,000; 1600 50,000; 1622 105,000; 1666 200,000; 1675 195,000; 1700 210,000;
1750 219,000; 1775 209,000; 1800 195,000; 1815 180,179; 1825 196,000; 1850 225,000; 1866 264,498; 1876 281,944;
1884 361,326; 1900 523,557; 1919 647,120; 1936 859,000; 1959 872,000; 1970 807,095; 1990 695,221; 2001 752,624;
2011 797,795; 2016 852,196; 2021 893,783; 2024 931,298; 2026 942,504. Values between anchors are log-interpolated.
**Note:** no opened source gave a figure for 1400 or 1500, so 1300–1600 is a pure interpolation (≈3,700 in 1400,
≈13,600 in 1500); this is plausible but not a sourced estimate. Chandler's figures (1675–1850, 1936) are urban estimates
mixed with municipal counts. 1940–1945 uses the interpolated value (wartime losses not modelled).

## Left out / simplified (uncertain)
- Old town hall (before 1652): no date or footprint found → only the fire on the Dam is shown.
- Blauwbrug, Portuguese Synagogue (1675; no coordinates found on opened pages), Anne Frank House: not drawn.
- Western islands (Bickers-, Realen-, Prinseneiland, 1610s) and Amsterdam-Noord's reclamation: no dates found → shown as
  land from the start. The western IJ polders (reclaimed by 1872) are shown as modern land throughout.
- Rokin north of Langebrugsteeg and the Dam stretch of the Amstel (filled later, date not checked) are land throughout;
  Damrak (north) and Rokin (south) are water throughout.
- Lakes dug in the 20th century (Sloterplas, Gaasperplas, Nieuwe Meer) come from OSM and show from the start.
- Canal widths are drawn at 32–40 m (real 20–40 m) so they read at this scale; digging dates within 1613–c.1625 and
  1660s are staggered by a few years (west → east, as the Canals article describes).
- Fill zones: Diemermeer 1629 (outline ≈), Eastern Islands 1661 (1660 extension), Haarlemmermeer 1852 (outline ≈),
  station islands 1884 (between 1876 site choice and 1889 opening), Eastern Docklands 1895/1915 (within 1874–1927),
  IJburg 2004 (work from 1997; the article says islands inhabited by 2004).
- District growth dates for the 19th-century belt (1877–1914), Noord, Buitenveldert, Zuidas and the suburbs are
  reconstructions; none of them is a dated claim on screen.
- Elevation: polders are below sea level, so the map is drawn flat (sea_level_m −30; water only from OSM + edits).
