# Toronto — research notes

Episode: ~14 min, 1650 → 2026 (Town of York 1793 → today). Research 11 Nov 2026. Every on-screen date/number below has
its source; all pages were opened during this run (30 pages, list in `_sources.json` / city.json `sources`). Landmark
coordinates are the published coordinates of each page (Wikipedia infobox) unless marked ≈. Street lines, district
outlines, the 1793 shoreline, landfill zones, open spaces, fire areas and the 1813 landing camp are simplified traces
(≈). Pages that could NOT be opened this run (PROVENANCE_REQUIRED after a .com→.org redirect): en.wikipedia.org
"History of Toronto", "Timeline of Toronto history", "Toronto Bay"; citypopulation.de (robots). Mirrors of the same
Wikipedia articles were used instead (IPFS, Kiwix, Wikiwand). The IPFS "History of Toronto" copy was read verbatim only for
its first ~40,000 characters; later facts from it (1950s onward) come from a model summary and are used only where
another opened page agrees or for wording-level context (expressways, Hazel), noted below.

Short names: HT = History of Toronto (IPFS mirror; HTk = Kiwix mini copy); YUC = York, Upper Canada (WP); OT = Old Toronto
(IPFS); DEM = Demographics of Toronto (WP); TH = Toronto Harbour (IPFS); TI = Toronto Islands (Wikiwand); OLA = Legislative
Assembly of Ontario pages.

## Shoreline and made land (≈)

- 1793 shore: "low sandy bluffs, just south of today's Front Street" (TH); Fort York's "original shoreline embankment is
  still visible outside the southern ramparts", fort 900 m inland by the 1920s (Fort York WP); the north shore moved
  ~500 m south overall (TH). Traced ≈ just south of Front Street.
- Fill zones (water until the year, then land; clipped to modern land): Esplanade 1865 (TH); central and western
  waterfront 1925 (second landfill from 1910 south of Lake Shore Blvd, Harbour Commission plan 1912 Humber–Woodbine; TH);
  Ashbridge's Bay west 1922 / east 1929 (Port Lands WP); Yonge–Don north-shore fill 1955 ("1950s", TH); Leslie Street
  Spit 1972 (≈, see above).
- Peninsula/island: the Eastern Gap is land until the 13 April 1858 storm (TI). The sand bar along the south of
  Ashbridge's Bay is kept as land (≈ position).
- Not drawn: the 1910 new Western Gap and the 1930s Hanlan's Bay infill for the airport (shape not known well enough).
