# New York — research notes

Episode: ~9 min, new format (RUNBOOK G), 1600 (Lenape Mannahatta) → 1624 (first Dutch settlers) → 2026. Every
on-screen date/number below has its source; all pages were opened during research on 21 Nov 2026 (web search first,
then the search-result URL opened). Full list in `_sources.json` / city.json `sources`.

## Population on screen

- No figure before 1628 (Lenape population not estimated in the sources opened).
- Anchors: 1628 270 (FA, NA); 1655 1,500 (NA: 1,500 of New Netherland's 2,000+ in the city); 1664 2,000
  (**midpoint**: FA gives 1,500, NA infobox 2,500); FA's "about 500 in 1660" contradicts NA's 1655 figure → not used.
- Colonial censuses (DEM table): 1698 4,937; 1712 5,840; 1723 7,248; 1737 10,664; 1746 11,717; 1756 13,046;
  1771 21,863.
- 1771 → 1790: no figure for the war years; the counter is interpolated 21,863 → 33,131 (note: the city emptied
  and refilled during the occupation; the sources opened give no number).
- US censuses, city as it then was (DCP-H): 1790 33,131; 1800 60,515; 1810 96,373; 1820 123,706; 1830 202,589;
  1840 312,710; 1850 515,547; 1860 813,669; 1870 942,292; 1880 1,206,299; 1890 1,515,301.
- 1890–1897 held at 1,515,301 (no old-city figure after 1890). Consolidation segment 1898–1900 runs 1,515,301 →
  3,437,202 (the jump is the five boroughs joining; DCP-H footnote: 1790–1890 totals are for the earlier city).
- Five boroughs (DCP-H / DH): 1900 3,437,202; 1910 4,766,883; 1920 5,620,048; 1930 6,930,446; 1940 7,454,995;
  1950 7,891,957; 1960 7,783,314 (DCP-H; DH 7,781,984); 1970 7,894,798 (DCP-H; DH 7,894,862); 1980 7,071,639;
  1990 7,322,564; 2000 8,008,278; 2010 8,175,133; 2020 8,804,190; 2024 8,478,072 (DCP-25); 2025 8,584,629 (DEM
  lead, Census estimate July 2025) — held for 2026 as the latest official estimate.
- Between anchors: log-interpolation.
- People notes ("≈ N people lived here" at 1925/1955/1985): engine reads the counter → interpolated census values.
