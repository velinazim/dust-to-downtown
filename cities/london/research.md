## Simplifications and uncertainties

- Roman wall path ≈ traced through the published gate sites (Tower Hill, Aldgate, Bishopsgate, Moorgate, St Alphage/Cripplegate fort,
  Aldersgate, Newgate, Ludgate, Blackfriars); riverside wall ≈ ~60–100 m inland of today's bank (the Roman waterfront lay further north).
  The 1284–1320 Blackfriars re-routing is not shown.
- The riverside wall's end date is unknown ("no evidence of post-Roman restoration"); it fades out by 700.
- Early Londinium, Lundenwic, medieval suburbs, Tudor growth, West End, Victorian/interwar/outer suburbs: ≈ areas for symbols, not surveyed boundaries.
- Great Fire area ≈ from Pudding Lane west to Fetter Lane/Temple, north towards the wall, east to near the Tower (NWE: halted near the Tower; ended at Pye Corner).
- Crystal Palace placed on the south side of Hyde Park (the source's point 51.5085, -0.1654 is a park-centre pin).
- Docks are ≈ rectangles; London Docks and Surrey Commercial Docks are shown filled in by 1970.
- Rivers Fleet and Walbrook (buried) and the Thames' pre-embankment width are not modelled.
- Buckingham Palace (1837) and the Millennium Dome (1999), Olympic Stadium (2011–12) dates are common knowledge, not from an opened source.
