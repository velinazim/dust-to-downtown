# Boston — research notes

Episode: ~14 min, 1625 → 2026. Every on-screen date/number below has its source (all pages opened during research,
1 Nov 2026; full list in `_sources.json` / city.json `sources`). Landmark coordinates are the published coordinates of
each page (Wikipedia infobox unless noted). Shorelines, fill zones, districts, fire areas and roads are simplified traces
between sourced points (marked ≈). The 1630-shoreline datasets (Leventhal Center "Historic Boston Shoreline",
CTPS 1630 shoreline map, OpenHistoricalMap) could not be downloaded from this session (host blocked), so the 1630 coast
is traced by hand from the descriptions below and the modern map; expect errors of ~100 m.

## Land, water and landfill (≈)
- Base map = modern land (AWS terrain + OSM rivers). Water that was filled later is drawn as `timed_water` with `fill: true`;
  each fill zone = modern land inside a rough zone polygon minus the traced 1630 land (Shawmut, Charlestown, South Boston =
  Dorchester Neck, Noddle's Island). Zones split by sourced fill periods:
  Mill Pond 1807–c.1828 (two halves: ≈1817, 1828); Long Wharf strip 1710–1721 (shown 1716); Mill Dam strip 1821; railroad
  causeway strips 1835; Quincy Market land by 1825; South Cove 1833–1845 (≈1840 / 1845 halves); South End 1830s–1870s
  (≈1850 north / 1870 south); Back Bay 1857–1881 in five east→west strips (1861, 1866, 1871, 1876, 1881 — linear between the
  sourced start and "Massachusetts Ave by 1881", per-block years not in the sources); Back Bay south (rail yards) by 1882 ≈;
  Kenmore 1890; Fens / Roxbury flats 1900; Atlantic Avenue waterfront by 1873 (cut through the wharves late 1860s + 1872 rubble);
  Fort Point (Boston Wharf Co. from the 1830s, flats being filled by 1845, ≈1860 / 1880); Commonwealth Flats ≈1900 and outer
  South Boston flats ≈1915 ("after 1880 landmaking accelerated"; South Boston 580 → ~1,600 acres); Castle Island link 1928;
  Logan 1923 (first airfield ≈ Jeffries Point area), then ≈1950 and ≈1960 for the 1940s–1950s fill.
- Not in sources, reconstructions inside a sourced range: Town Cove inner wharves ≈1760 (wharves from 1708 on), North End
  waterfront ≈1840, West Cove / West End and Charles St / Public Garden / Bay Village land ≈1830 (Beacon Hill and Mt Vernon cut
  in the early 1800s; Public Garden laid out 1860 per Timeline), North Station flats ≈1850 (Pemberton Hill cut 1835 to fill
  flats near today's North Station), Charlestown waterfront ≈1880 and East Cambridge ≈1850, Cambridge riverbank / MIT site
  ≈1895 (WBUR: the 1895 map shows Cambridge had filled the estuary where MIT stands; Charlestown and South Boston expanded
  1796–1895), East Boston waterfront ≈1870, South Bay ≈1900.
- Each fill sheet is grown ~45 m over the water around it (never onto 1630 land or another zone) so the modern shoreline
  does not show through; Castle Island is kept as 1630 land (fort from 1634).
- Left out: Dorchester Bay / Columbia Point, Chelsea and the outer harbour shore stay as today's land from the start.
- The Neck is drawn ~150 m wide so it reads on screen (source: ~120 ft at high tide).
- Boston Neck gate and earthwork from c.1631 (strengthened 1710, 1774); removal year not in sources → drawn until ≈1830 when
  the South End fill began around it.

## Districts (≈)
First town around Dock Square / Cornhill from 1630; North End 1640–1700; South town 1660–1730; brick rebuild after 1711;
West End and Beacon Hill (Beacon Hill after the State House, 1795–1840); Charlestown village 1630 and town rebuilt after 1776;
Roxbury and Cambridge villages (founded 1630–31, not on screen); Bulfinch Triangle after the Mill Pond; South Cove, South End,
Back Bay (only after their fill dates; the engine delays buildings on fill until the fill year); South Boston from 1805
(annexed 1804 per the First Street sign), East Boston from 1835; suburbs 1850–1990; downtown offices after 1873; towers 1965+;
Seaport 2005–2024 (no source for exact dates; generic modern growth, not on screen).

## Fires, camps, smoke
1711 fire drawn only at the Town House (extent unknown). 1760 fire: Cornhill → Fort Hill / King St (≈ polygon). 1872 fire:
≈ polygon Summer/Washington/Milk to the waterfront, 65 acres. British camp on the Common 1768–1776 (Boston Common WP: camped
before the war). Siege camps at Cambridge and Roxbury 1775–1776. Smoke on Breed's Hill (June 1775), on Dorchester Heights
(March 1776), and in the North End (1919). Charlestown's burning in 1775 is not in the opened sources → not shown.
