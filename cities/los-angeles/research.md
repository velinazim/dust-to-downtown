## Simplifications

- District polygons are rough reconstructions of the built-up area by period (pueblo, 1850s town, 1880s boom city,
  annexed towns at their founding dates, basin/Westside/South Bay/San Gabriel Valley/Valley sprawl). They include
  neighbouring cities (Santa Monica, Beverly Hills, Pasadena, Long Beach…) because the timelapse shows the built-up
  area, not the city limits. Mountains (Santa Monica Mts/Griffith Park, Elysian hills, Verdugos, San Gabriel foothills)
  are kept unbuilt as simple polygons.
- The harbor uses the modern coastline (Terminal Island and the port's landfill exist from the start); the 1899
  breakwater and the river's concrete channel are not drawn. Lakes/reservoirs appear from 1900 (most are reservoirs).
- Not on screen because the opened pages disagree or were not readable: Exposition Park rename year (1910 vs 1913), 1938
  flood death toll, Pacific Electric founding, 1892 oil (summary-only), Northridge 1994 (death tolls disagree), the
  January 2025 fires (summary-only source).
- Sensitive: Yaanga's removal (1845/1847) and the Chavez Ravine evictions are stated plainly, without judgement.
